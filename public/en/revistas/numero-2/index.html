The system of combined agricultural insurance in Spain

In depth

Detailed analysis of the Spanish Combined Agricultural Insurance system by Ignacio Machetti Bermejo (President of Agroseguro), reviewing its historical background, public-private collaboration between ENESA, CCS, and Agroseguro, contracting and loss ratio trends, and the new premium formulation following CNMC and DGSFP recommendations.

The Norwegian natural catastrophe compensation system in the past and the future

Contributions

Analysis by Gunn Eide (Norwegian Agriculture Agency) of the historical background, evolution, and reform of Norway’s natural catastrophe compensation regime—combining the National Natural Disaster Fund and mandatory private natural perils insurance—and its future priorities regarding climate change and risk management.

Analysis by Miguel Luque Herrán (Insurance Director at REPSOL) on how an international company identifies, assesses, mitigates, and insures natural risks under the ISO 31.000 framework, detailing key technical policy clauses and the role of public coverage systems.

Reviews
