The insured interest and distinguishing it from the insured risk

Case law

In general contract theory, consent, consideration and the subject-matter are the three essential elements. In an insurance contract, the consideration is the insured risk, whereas the subject-matter is the insured interest. In both cases, if they are not present, the Insurance Contracts Act (LCS) declares the contract null and void.
