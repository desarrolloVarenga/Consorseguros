Who is responsible for processing your data?

In accordance with the (EU) Regulation 2016/679 by the European Parliament and Council, of the 27th of April, 2016 (GDPR) and with the rest of the applicable legislation on the subject of personal data protection, we would like to inform you that any personal data that we manage, will be incorporated to the processing that makes up the register of activities for the body in charge of the processing, CONSORCIO DE COMPENSACIÓN DE SEGUROS E.P.E., with Fiscal Identification code Q2826011E, telephone number +34 91 339 55 00, and address at Paseo de la Castellana nº 32, 28046, Madrid (Spain).

Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros has designed a Data Protection Officer, that can be reached through the e-mail address: dpo@consorseguros.es.

Why do we process your personal data?

Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros will process your personal data, gathered through the webpage www.consorsegurosdigital.com, with any of the following purposes:

  • Contact: Attention to requests made by users through the contact link in the webpage.
  • Subscription: Management of the subscriptions and sending alerts about new issues of our digital magazine.

What is the legitimacy for processing your data?

The basis of the processing for the abovementioned purposes is the interested party’s consent –art. 6.1 a) GDPR-. The said content is given by sending a request through the contact link of our webpage or by applying for subscription to our digital magazine new issue alerts.

Who will we inform of your personal data?

Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros will not communicate your personal data to third parties, except to those third parties, bodies and public institutions of the National Government and the Regional and Local Administrations, including the jurisdictional bodies to which our Company is obliged to provide them.

Your data may be accessed by external technological service suppliers, such as accommodation, computer maintenance and other similar services, who provide their services to Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros, and who will process your data strictly following the instructions given by this organisation and under its responsibility.

How long will we retain your data?

The user’s personal data will be kept, during the following periods:

  • For the management of requests made through the website contact link: During the necessary time to manage your request and, later, during 6 years after your consultation.
  • For the management and sending alerts of new issues of our digital magazine: During the time in which your subscription to our alerts relative to the digital magazine is active and, later, during 6 years after you have unsubscribed.

What safety measures will be applied to your personal data?

The processing of the personal data provided will be carried out adopting the technical and organisational steps necessary to prevent the loss, misuse, alteration and unauthorised access to this data, in view of the state of the technology, the nature of the data and the risk analysis performed.

Do we perform international data transfers?

The user’s personal data won’t be object of international transfers.

How can I exercise my rights regarding my personal data?

If you wish to exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation, deletion, opposition, limitation, portability and right to not be the object of automated decisions, as well as revoking your consent, you may address our Data Protection Delegate by way of a written request to the postal address at Paseo de la Castellana nº 32, 28046, Madrid (Spain); or using the following email address dpo@consorseguros.es.

This request must include the interested party’s name; a copy of their national identity document, passport or other valid identification document and, if applicable, the same information of their representative, as well as the accrediting document of the representation, along with an address for notifications and specification of the object of the request.

In the case that the interested party considers that the abovementioned rights have not been attended to in accordance with the current legislation, the corresponding protection of rights may be brought before the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
